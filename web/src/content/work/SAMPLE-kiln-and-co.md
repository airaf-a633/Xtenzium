## The problem

Kiln's product photography was excellent and their store made it look cheap.
Worse, every collection launch meant emailing a freelancer and waiting a week.

## What we built

A full identity system — type, palette, art direction, and the rules for using
them — then an Astro storefront built on it. Behind it, a small admin where the
studio drafts a collection, drops in photos, and publishes.

## What it returned

Conversion rose 36% against the previous store. Launch cost went from a week of
freelancer time to an afternoon of their own.
